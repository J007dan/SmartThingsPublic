Five of the six stories below dominated a news cycle and moved almost nothing. The sixth barely trended for a day and is permanent. That inversion is the whole reason this app exists — the volume of a story on the day it breaks tells you almost nothing about whether it will matter, and the feeling of being informed is mostly the feeling of being upset on schedule.

Waiting two weeks costs you nothing. Nothing here was actionable on the day it broke. Every single item is more accurate now than it was then.

Prototype notes

Every story, date, and outcome here is real and sourced — researched by hand on 24 August 2026 for the 10 August edition. In the shipping version this is a pipeline: pull the archive for T-minus-14, cluster by story, then research each cluster's outcome before publishing.

Known gap: this edition skews toward one administration because that is what the 10 August cycle was. A real edition needs an explicit balance pass, or the app becomes the thing it is trying to cure.
