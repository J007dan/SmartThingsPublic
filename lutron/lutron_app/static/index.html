Scenes

Scenes live in this app and drive the processor over LEAP. Create as many as you like — unlike the Lutron app, there is no limit and no Designer visit required.

Schedules

Fire any scene at a fixed time, or relative to sunrise/sunset with an offset.

Processor scenes

Scenes and phantom buttons programmed into the processor itself with Lutron Designer. This app can trigger them but cannot create or edit them — LEAP has no write-configuration API.
